Use the SAME Gmail as step 1

The photo at the top right of Play Store is your Gmail. It must be the one that joined the group in step 1 — tap the photo to switch if it isn't. Otherwise the app won’t show.

Play Store में ऊपर दाईं ओर की फ़ोटो = आपका Gmail। ये वही होना चाहिए जिससे Step 1 में ग्रुप जॉइन किया।

Open the app and follow it

The app names your phone and shows each setting it needs, one at a time — with a picture of exactly what to tap.
Do each one. The app opens when all are done.
